About BMI Calculator

Free BMI calculator to check your Body Mass Index. Supports metric (kg/cm) and imperial (lbs/inches) units with visual health status indicators.

Features

  • ✓ Metric and Imperial support
  • ✓ Instant calculation
  • ✓ Color-coded health status category

Key Benefits

  • ✅ Instant health check metric
  • ✅ Supports both metric and imperial units
  • ✅ Visual color indicators for easy reading

How to use BMI Calculator

  1. Select your preferred measurement units (Metric or Imperial).
  2. Enter your height in the specified unit.
  3. Enter your weight in the specified unit.
  4. Click "Calculate BMI".
  5. View your BMI score and the corresponding health category.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a normal BMI? ▼

A normal, healthy BMI is generally considered to be between 18.5 and 24.9.

Is BMI accurate for everyone? ▼

BMI is a general guideline. It may not be accurate for athletes with high muscle mass, pregnant women, or the elderly.
