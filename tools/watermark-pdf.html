About Watermark PDF

Easily add a custom text watermark to all pages of your PDF document. Free, secure, and fully private.

Features

  • Diagonal placement
  • Applies to all pages
  • Client-side processing

Key Benefits

  • Protect your documents
  • 100% local processing
  • Customizable text

How to use Watermark PDF

  1. Select a PDF file
  2. Enter your watermark text
  3. Click Add Watermark and download
