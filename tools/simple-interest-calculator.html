About Simple Interest

Calculate simple interest without compounding. View your total principal + interest effortlessly.

Features

How to use Simple Interest

  1. Enter the Principal Amount.
  2. Enter the Annual Interest Rate.
  3. Enter Time in years.
  4. Click Calculate.
