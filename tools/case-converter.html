About Case Converter

Free online tool to convert text to UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, camelCase, snake_case, and more.

Features

  • 8+ case formatting options
  • Developer-friendly cases (snake_case, camelCase)
  • One-click copy

Key Benefits

  • Quickly formats text to desired case
  • Helps maintain consistent styling
  • Saves time over manual retyping

How to use Case Converter

  1. Paste your text into the input field.
  2. Click on the formatting button corresponding to the case you want (e.g., UPPERCASE, lowercase, camelCase).
  3. The converted text will appear in the result box.
  4. Click "Copy Result" to copy it to your clipboard.
