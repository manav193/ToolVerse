About GPA Calculator

Easily calculate your college or high school GPA by entering your grades and credit hours.

Features

  • Dynamic course rows
  • Standard 4.0 scale
  • Total credits summary

Key Benefits

  • Monitor academic progress
  • Target desired GPAs
  • Quick and easy to use

How to use GPA Calculator

  1. Click "Add Course" for more rows if needed.
  2. Enter course credits and select a grade.
  3. Click Calculate GPA.

Frequently Asked Questions

This calculator uses a standard unweighted 4.0 scale (A=4, B=3, C=2, D=1, F=0).
