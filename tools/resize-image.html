About Image Resizer

Free online image resizer. Change image dimensions quickly and securely right in your browser. No upload to server required.

Features

  • Client-side processing (100% Private)
  • Aspect ratio locking
  • Drag & drop support

Key Benefits

  • Fast client-side processing without uploads
  • Maintains original aspect ratio easily
  • Perfect for meeting image size requirements

How to use Image Resizer

  1. Drag and drop an image into the dashed upload area, or click to browse files.
  2. View the original dimensions of your uploaded image.
  3. Enter the new Width or Height. Keep "Lock Aspect Ratio" checked to avoid distortion.
  4. Click "Resize & Download" to save the resized image.
