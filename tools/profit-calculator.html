About Profit Calculator

Calculate gross profit, margin, and markup from cost and selling price. Essential tool for sellers.

Features

  • Margin percentage

  1. Enter the Cost Price of the item.
  2. Enter the Selling Price.
  3. View the calculated profit, margin, and markup.

Frequently Asked Questions

Margin is profit as a percentage of the selling price. Markup is profit as a percentage of the cost price.
