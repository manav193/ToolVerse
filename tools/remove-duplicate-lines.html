About Remove Duplicate Lines

Easily remove duplicate lines from your text. Sort alphabetically and trim whitespace. Free online list cleaner tool.

Features

  • ✓ Case sensitive option
  • ✓ Trim whitespace option
  • ✓ Alphabetical sorting
  • ✓ Instant processing

Key Benefits

  • ✅ Cleans up messy data lists
  • ✅ Ensures unique items for accurate processing
  • ✅ Easy sorting and formatting

How to use Remove Duplicate Lines

  1. Paste your list of items (one per line) into the input area.
  2. Select options such as Case-sensitive, Trim whitespace, or Sort alphabetically.
  3. Click the "Remove Duplicates" button.
  4. Copy your clean, unique list from the result box.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "Trim whitespace" do? ▼

It removes extra spaces at the beginning and end of each line before checking for duplicates.

Can it sort the output? ▼

Yes! Just check the "Sort alphabetically" option before processing.
