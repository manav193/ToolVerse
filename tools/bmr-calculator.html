About BMR Calculator

Find your BMR to understand how many calories your body burns at rest. Essential for diet planning.

Features

  • ✓ Calculates using Mifflin-St Jeor
  • ✓ Calculates using Harris-Benedict
  • ✓ Detailed breakdown

Key Benefits

  • ✅ Understand resting energy expenditure
  • ✅ Base metric for diet plans
  • ✅ Compare equation models

How to use BMR Calculator

  1. Select Gender and Age.
  2. Enter Height in cm and Weight in kg.
  3. Click Calculate BMR.
