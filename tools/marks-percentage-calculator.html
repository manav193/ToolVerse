About Marks Percentage Calculator

Easily calculate your test score percentage and letter grade by entering marks obtained and total marks.

Features

  • Accurate decimal calculation
  • Letter grade mapping
  • Simple interface

Key Benefits

  • Instantly see test percentage
  • Identify letter grade
  • Great for teachers and students

How to use Marks Percentage Calculator

  1. Enter the marks you obtained.
  2. Enter the total possible marks.
  3. Click Calculate Percentage.
