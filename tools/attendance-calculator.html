About Attendance Calculator

Free attendance calculator for students. Enter your total classes and attended classes to see how many more you need to attend to reach your target percentage.

Features

  • ✓ Calculates classes needed to attend
  • ✓ Calculates classes safe to skip
  • ✓ Color-coded visual feedback

Key Benefits

  • ✅ Helps students track attendance effortlessly
  • ✅ Calculates safe skips
  • ✅ Prevents falling below target percentages

How to use Attendance Calculator

  1. Enter the total number of classes held so far.
  2. Enter the number of classes you have attended.
  3. Set your target attendance percentage (e.g., 75%).
  4. Click "Calculate" to see your current percentage and how many classes you must attend (or can skip) to meet your target.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the required classes calculated? ▼

It mathematically determines the exact number of consecutive classes you must attend to make the ratio (Attended/Total) hit your target percentage.
