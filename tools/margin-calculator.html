About Margin Calculator

Calculate the required selling price to achieve your desired gross margin. Perfect for retail pricing.

Features

  • Selling price calculation
  • Gross profit calculation
  • Markup reference

Key Benefits

  • Helps set retail prices accurately
  • Shows actual profit amount
  • Simple and reliable

How to use Margin Calculator

  1. Enter your item Cost.
  2. Enter your desired Gross Margin percentage.
  3. Click Calculate to see the required Selling Price.

Frequently Asked Questions
